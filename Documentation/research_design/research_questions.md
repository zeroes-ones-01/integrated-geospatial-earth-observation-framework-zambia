# Research Questions

## 1. Overarching Research Question

How can agricultural productivity statistics, climate information, agricultural greenhouse-gas emissions data, geospatial datasets and Earth observation products be integrated within a reproducible framework to support the assessment of agricultural productivity and climate vulnerability in Zambia?

## 2. Specific Research Questions

**RQ1. Data integration and management**

How can agricultural, climatic, emissions and geographical datasets from different sources be harmonised and integrated into a relational database while preserving data provenance, consistency and integrity?

**RQ2. Agricultural productivity patterns**

What temporal patterns are evident in crop production, harvested area and crop yields in Zambia between 1991 and 2020, both at the aggregate level and across the selected crops?

**RQ3. Climate and agricultural productivity**

What descriptive relationships are observable between national annual precipitation, temperature and agricultural productivity over the study period, and what limitations affect the interpretation of these relationships?

**RQ4. Agricultural greenhouse-gas emissions**

What temporal patterns are evident in the selected agricultural greenhouse-gas emissions categories between 1991 and 2020, and what descriptive comparisons with agricultural production can be made where the indicators have compatible definitions, units and coverage?

**RQ5. Geospatial and Earth observation integration**

How can geospatial datasets and appropriately selected Earth observation products be incorporated into the existing database and analytical framework to improve the geographical and environmental representation of agricultural conditions in Zambia?

**RQ6. Subnational environmental assessment**

How can spatially explicit indicators of climate exposure and agricultural sensitivity be used to conduct a preliminary screening of areas requiring further investigation, and to what extent can reliable adaptive-capacity information be incorporated into a broader assessment of agricultural climate vulnerability?

## 3. Analytical Focus

The questions are addressed through complementary stages:

- **Data management:** source identification, data harmonisation, relational database design, validation and documentation.
- **National statistical analysis:** temporal trends and descriptive comparisons of agricultural productivity, climate variables and selected emissions indicators.
- **Geospatial integration:** preparation and integration of geographical datasets and Earth observation products.
- **Spatial assessment:** examination of spatial patterns in agricultural and environmental conditions, subject to data availability, spatial resolution and validation.

## 4. Alignment with the Current Project Status

The first four research questions are supported by the existing national statistical database and its current analytical components. The fifth and sixth questions define the intended geospatial and Earth observation extension.

The latter questions should not be treated as completed research findings. Their investigation requires the acquisition, processing and validation of suitable spatial datasets and satellite-derived products.

## 5. Interpretative Boundaries

The research questions are deliberately framed to distinguish descriptive statistical evidence from spatial assessment and causal explanation.

National annual climate observations cannot establish local exposure, seasonal rainfall distribution or the occurrence and intensity of individual drought and heat events. Similarly, national productivity trends cannot independently explain the effects of climate conditions on particular farming communities or locations.

The selected emissions indicators do not constitute a complete agricultural greenhouse-gas inventory. Any comparisons between emissions and production must account for differences in source definitions, units, coverage and temporal aggregation.

The project will therefore distinguish clearly between implemented analyses, planned investigations and conclusions that cannot be supported by the available evidence.
