# Analytical Figures

## Overview

This directory contains 12 national-level analytical figures for *An Integrated Geospatial Database and Earth Observation Framework for Assessing Agricultural Productivity, Climate Vulnerability and Agricultural Greenhouse-Gas Emissions in Zambia*. The figures cover annual observations for Zambia from 1991 to 2020. Each figure is available as PNG and SVG.

## Crop coverage and aggregate yield

The 11 crops are maize, cassava, sorghum, soybeans, groundnuts, millet, sweet potatoes, wheat, rice, beans and sunflower.

Annual production and harvested area are summed across these crops. Aggregate yield is calculated as:

`Aggregate yield = Total production / Total harvested area`

This is a production-weighted aggregate, not the arithmetic mean of crop-level yields.

## Climate analysis

The national annual rainfall and mean-temperature series cover 1991–2020. The underlying climate dataset is CRU TS v4.10, obtained through the World Bank Climate Change Knowledge Portal.

The scatter plots include fitted linear regression lines. Pearson correlations are:

- Rainfall and aggregate crop yield: **r = 0.4106**
- Mean temperature and aggregate crop yield: **r = -0.1606**

These values describe associations in the observed annual data. They do not demonstrate causation or control for other influences on agricultural productivity.

## Agricultural emissions and intensity

The emissions analysis includes four categories: Burning - Crop residues; Crop Residues; Rice Cultivation; and Synthetic Fertilizers.

The emissions-intensity indicator is calculated as:

`Emissions intensity = Included emissions (kt CO₂e) × 1,000,000 / Selected-crop production (tonnes)`

The unit is kg CO₂e per tonne of selected-crop production. This is a scoped indicator based on the four included emissions categories and the production of the 11 selected crops. It is not a comprehensive national agricultural greenhouse-gas inventory or a complete national agricultural emissions-intensity measure.

## Data and analytical scope

The project uses processed agricultural, climate and emissions datasets assembled from FAOSTAT and World Bank sources. The climate series is identified separately above.

These figures represent national annual observations. They do not provide provincial or district-level estimates, spatially explicit climate exposure or satellite-derived measurements. They document the completed national-level analytical component; geospatial and Earth observation integration remain planned extensions and should not be described as completed.

## Reproducibility

The figures are generated by `analysis/python/generate_analytical_figures.py`. From the project root, run:

```powershell
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe analysis/python/generate_analytical_figures.py
```

The script validates input datasets before generation. Its export function preserves existing figure files rather than automatically overwriting them. Intentional regeneration should follow the project's review and approval procedure.

## Limitations

1. The analysis covers 30 annual observations from 1991 to 2020.
2. Crop analysis is restricted to the 11 selected crops.
3. Climate observations are national aggregates and do not represent local spatial variability.
4. Correlations and fitted regression lines do not establish causal effects.
5. The emissions analysis covers four specified categories, not all agricultural greenhouse-gas sources.
6. Emissions intensity uses selected-crop production as its denominator and must be interpreted within that scope.
7. Spatially explicit analysis and Earth observation integration have not yet been completed.
